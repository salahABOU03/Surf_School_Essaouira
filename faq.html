Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about watersports lessons, conditions, and activities in Essaouira.

General Questions

How do I book a lesson?

Simply send us a WhatsApp message at +212 625 044 412. Tell us your preferred sport, level, dates, and group size. We speak English, French, and Arabic. We'll confirm your booking within minutes!

Do I need prior experience?

No experience needed! All our courses are designed for complete beginners. Our certified instructors guide you step by step from the very first moment. Prior board sports experience (surfing, skateboarding, snowboarding) can help but is absolutely not required.

What equipment is included in lessons?

All lessons include full equipment and wetsuit. For wingfoil: wing, board, foil, helmet, impact vest. For kitesurf: kite, bar, board, harness, helmet. For windsurf: board, sail, mast, boom, harness. For surf: surfboard and wetsuit. You just bring swimwear and sunscreen!

Can you pick me up from my hotel?

Yes! We offer free pick-up within Essaouira city. We'll drive you directly to the best beach spot for your session and drop you back after. Just let us know your accommodation when booking.

What languages do your instructors speak?

Our instructors speak English, French, and Arabic fluently. We can provide instruction in any of these languages.

Do you offer private lessons?

Yes! We offer both private (1-on-1) and group lessons for all sports. Private lessons from 55€/hour offer faster progression with personalized attention. See our pricing page for all options.

Can I rent equipment without a lesson?

Yes! Experienced riders can rent equipment for 35€ per half-day — wingfoil, kitesurf, windsurf, or surf. We'll verify your level before rental for safety.

Wingfoil Questions

Where to learn wingfoil in Essaouira?

Kawki Kite is the leading wingfoil school in Essaouira. We teach at Essaouira Bay, which offers flat water and consistent side-shore wind — the perfect conditions for learning wing foiling safely.

Is Essaouira good for wingfoil?

Essaouira is one of the best wingfoil destinations in the world. The bay offers consistent 15–25 knot winds, flat water, and a sandy bottom. Plus, wingfoil requires less wind than kitesurf, so even lighter days are rideable.

How to start wingfoil?

Book a beginner wingfoil lesson! We start with ground handling on the beach, then practice balance on the board in the water, then water starts, and finally first flights. Most beginners are foiling within 6–8 hours of lessons.

What is the best wingfoil school in Morocco?

Kawki Kite is rated 4.9/5 with 127+ reviews and is considered one of the best wingfoil schools in Morocco. We offer certified instruction, the latest equipment, and personalized coaching at Essaouira's prime spot.

Kitesurf Questions

Where to learn kitesurf in Essaouira?

Kawki Kite offers IKO certified kitesurf lessons at Essaouira Bay. The side-shore wind and sandy bottom make it one of the safest places in the world to learn kitesurfing.

Is Essaouira good for kitesurf?

Essaouira is the "Wind City of Africa" — one of the world's best kitesurfing destinations. 300+ wind days per year, consistent 18–30 knot trade winds, flat water, and affordable prices.

How to start kitesurf?

Book a beginner kitesurf course! Our IKO program covers safety, kite control, body drag, water start, and first rides. Most students ride independently after 8–12 hours of instruction.

What is the best season for kitesurf in Morocco?

April to September is the best kitesurf season in Essaouira. Peak winds of 18–30 knots blow daily from June through August. Check our live weather page for current conditions.

What is the best kite school in Morocco?

Kawki Kite in Essaouira is one of the top-rated kite schools in Morocco. IKO certified instructors, modern equipment, safe teaching environment, and great value. 4.9/5 from 127+ reviews.

Windsurf & Surf Questions

Where to learn windsurf in Essaouira?

Kawki Kite offers windsurf lessons at Essaouira Bay. The consistent trade winds and flat water make it one of the best windsurfing spots in Morocco.

What is the best surf school in Essaouira?

Kawki Kite offers surf lessons from just 25€/hour at Essaouira's best breaks. Our instructors know the local waves and choose the best spot for your level each day.

How to learn windsurf?

Our windsurf lessons start with theory and rig setup, then balance and uphauling on the water, then basic steering and tacking. Most beginners can sail independently after 4–6 hours of instruction.

Location & Conditions

What is the best wind season in Essaouira?

The main wind season is April to September, with strongest winds in June–August (20–35 knots daily). The Alizé trade winds are remarkably consistent during this period. Check our live weather page for today's conditions.

What are the best spots for watersports near Essaouira?

The three main spots are: Essaouira Bay (all levels, flat water), Sidi Kaouki (intermediate+ surf/kite, bigger waves), and Moulay Bouzerktoun (advanced kite/windsurf, wave riding). See our full spots guide.

What are the best activities in Essaouira?

Essaouira offers incredible watersports (wingfoil, kitesurf, windsurf, surf), plus a beautiful UNESCO medina, fresh seafood, camel riding, and the famous Gnaoua music festival. See our activity guide.
