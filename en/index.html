Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking your lessons in Essaouira.

Where to learn wingfoil in Essaouira?

Kaouki Kite teaches wingfoil directly in Essaouira bay, the best spot with flat water, sandy bottom and consistent wind. We offer lessons for all levels.

What is the best season for kitesurfing in Morocco?

April to September offers the strongest and most consistent trade winds (18–30 knots daily). However, Essaouira enjoys rideable wind virtually year-round. Check our live weather forecast.

Do I need experience to start?

No prior experience needed! All our lessons are designed for complete beginners. Our certified instructors guide you step by step in complete safety.
