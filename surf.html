Surfing in Essaouira — Morocco's Atlantic Playground

While Essaouira is famous for its wind, the region also offers excellent surf conditions. The Atlantic Ocean delivers consistent swells that create perfect beach breaks for beginners, while nearby spots like Sidi Kaouki and the coast south of town offer larger waves for experienced surfers.

Kawki Kite offers affordable surf lessons starting from just 25€ per hour — the most accessible way to experience watersports in Essaouira. Our experienced surf instructors know the local breaks intimately and will take you to the best waves for your level.

Surf FAQ

Is Essaouira good for surfing?

Essaouira offers excellent beginner-friendly surf conditions with consistent Atlantic swells and sandy beach breaks. The water temperature is pleasant year-round (16–22°C). For bigger waves, Sidi Kaouki (25 min south) is one of Morocco's best surf spots.

What is the best season for surfing in Essaouira?

Surfing in Essaouira is great year-round. Winter (October–March) brings bigger swells perfect for experienced surfers. Summer offers gentler waves ideal for beginners. No matter when you visit, you'll find surfable conditions.

Can I surf on windy days in Essaouira?

Very windy days can make surfing challenging as the wind chops up the waves. On these days, we recommend trying wingfoil, kitesurf, or windsurf instead — sports that thrive in the wind! Early mornings before the thermal wind kicks in are often the best time for surf.
