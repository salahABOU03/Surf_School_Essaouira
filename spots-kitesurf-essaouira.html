Best Watersports Spots in Essaouira

Your complete guide to Essaouira Bay, Sidi Kaouki, Moulay Bouzerktoun and the best beaches for water sports and activities in Morocco.

Essaouira Bay

The main spot for all water sports in Essaouira. This vast, crescent-shaped bay offers flat water, consistent side-shore wind, and a long sandy beach. It's the safest and most popular location for wingfoil, kitesurf, and windsurf lessons. Beginners love the gradual sandy entry and the sheltered inner bay area.

Sidi Kaouki

Located 25 km south of Essaouira, Sidi Kaouki is a stunning, less crowded beach with bigger waves and stronger wind. It's a favorite for intermediate and advanced surfers seeking more powerful waves, and for experienced kiters looking for wave riding conditions. The beach village has a laid-back, boho atmosphere.

Surf Kitesurf Windsurf Intermediate+

Moulay Bouzerktoun

Located 25 km north of Essaouira, "Moulay" is a world-renowned spot for advanced kitesurfing and windsurfing. The combination of strong wind and clean Atlantic swell creates perfect conditions for wave riding and big air. This spot has hosted international competitions and attracts pros from around the world.

Things to Do in Essaouira

Beyond watersports, Essaouira is a UNESCO World Heritage city with a rich history, stunning medina, vibrant art scene, and incredible food. Here are some of the best activities and things to do:

  • Explore the Medina — Wander through the ancient walled city with its blue-and-white architecture, art galleries, and craft shops
  • Visit the Port — Watch fishermen bring in the day's catch and enjoy fresh seafood at the port stalls
  • Camel riding on the beach — A classic Essaouira experience along the long sandy coastline
  • Gnaoua Music Festival — The annual Gnaoua World Music Festival draws artists from around the globe (June)
  • Sunset at Skala — Watch the sunset from the historic ramparts overlooking the Atlantic
  • Day trip to Sidi Kaouki — Visit the tranquil beach village for surfing and relaxation
  • Argan oil workshops — Discover the region's famous argan oil production

Essaouira Beach Activities

Essaouira's vast sandy beach is the heart of outdoor activity in the city. Whether you're looking for adventure sports like wingfoil and kitesurf, or simply want to relax on the sand, the beach has something for everyone:

  • Wingfoil, kitesurf, windsurf, and surf lessons & rental
  • Stand-up paddleboarding (SUP)
  • Beach horse riding and camel rides
  • Beach football and volleyball
  • Yoga and meditation on the sand
  • Beachside cafés and restaurants
