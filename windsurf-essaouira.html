Master the timeless art of windsurfing in Morocco's legendary wind capital. Courses for all levels, equipment included.

Windsurfing in Essaouira — A Classic Experience

Long before kitesurfing and wingfoiling arrived, Essaouira was already world-famous for windsurfing. The consistent Alizé trade winds that funnel through the bay create ideal conditions for this timeless sport. Whether you're discovering windsurfing for the first time or a seasoned sailor looking to ride the legendary Essaouira wind, Kaouki Kite has you covered.

Windsurfing offers a unique, direct connection between rider, sail, and ocean. The sensation of planing across flat water powered by the wind is exhilarating and addictive. And Essaouira is one of the best places in the world to experience it.

Beginner Windsurf Course

  • Theory & rig setup — Understand wind theory and how to assemble your equipment
  • Uphauling & balance — Pull up the sail and find your stance on the board
  • Basic steering — Learn to control direction using the sail position
  • First tacks — Turn the board and ride back to where you started

Intermediate Windsurf Course

  • Harness use — Hook in and use your body weight for power
  • Planing — Get the board up to speed and feel it lift onto the plane
  • Footstraps — Secure your feet for high-speed control
  • Waterstarts — Get back on the board without uphauling

Advanced Windsurf Coaching

  • High-wind sailing — Master strong Essaouira conditions
  • Jibing at speed — Smooth carving turns while planing
  • Wave sailing — Ride the swells and surf the waves under sail
  • Freestyle basics — Introduction to Vulcans and Spocks

Windsurf FAQ

Is Essaouira good for windsurfing?

Essaouira is a legendary windsurfing destination, having hosted professional windsurfing competitions for decades. The consistent Alizé trade winds of 15–30 knots and the flat bay water create ideal conditions for all levels.

Can beginners windsurf in Essaouira?

Absolutely! Our beginner windsurf lessons take place in the sheltered area of the bay where the wind is gentler. We use large, stable boards and small sails to make learning comfortable and fun.

What is included in windsurf lessons?

All windsurf lessons include board, sail, mast, boom, harness, and wetsuit. We select equipment size based on your weight and the conditions to ensure optimal learning.
