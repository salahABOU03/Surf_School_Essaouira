Kitesurfing in Essaouira — Morocco's Wind Capital

Essaouira is known worldwide as the "Wind City of Africa", and for good reason. The Alizé trade winds funnel through the bay with remarkable consistency, creating one of the most reliable kitesurfing destinations on the planet. With 300+ wind days per year, flat water in the bay, and a safe sandy beach, it's no wonder kitesurfers from around the world flock here.

At Kaouki Kite, we offer kite lessons for every level — from complete beginners who have never touched a kite to advanced riders seeking to perfect their technique. Our IKO certified instructors ensure your safety while maximizing your progression.

Beginner Kitesurf Course (0–12 hours)

  • Safety briefing & theory — Understand wind window, kite setup, and safety systems
  • Kite control on the beach — Master launching, flying, and landing the trainer kite
  • Body dragging — Get pulled through the water by the kite, learn power control
  • Water starts & first rides — Stand on the board and ride your first meters

Intermediate Kitesurf (12–30 hours)

  • Riding upwind — Maintain and gain ground against the wind
  • Transitions — Smooth turns and direction changes
  • Jumping basics — Your first air time and safe landings
  • Self-rescue — Full autonomy on the water

Advanced Kitesurf Coaching

  • Big air & megaloops — Maximize height and rotations
  • Wave riding — Kite in the surf at Moulay Bouzerktoun
  • Strapless riding — Surfboard-style kitesurfing
  • Foil kiting — Transition to a kite hydrofoil

Kitesurf FAQ

Where is the best place to learn kitesurf in Essaouira?

Essaouira Bay is the best spot for kitesurf beginners. The side-shore wind, flat water, and sandy bottom create the safest conditions. For intermediate and advanced riders, Moulay Bouzerktoun offers wave riding opportunities.

What is the best season for kitesurf in Morocco?

The best kitesurf season in Essaouira runs from April to September, with peak winds of 18–30 knots in June through August. Water temperature is pleasant at 18–22°C. Winter months still offer rideable days, especially for experienced kiters.

How long does it take to learn kitesurfing?

Most beginners can ride independently after 8–12 hours of structured lessons. The IKO program we follow ensures safe and efficient progression. Athletic students with prior board sports experience may progress faster.

Is Essaouira good for kitesurfing beginners?

Essaouira is one of the best places in the world to learn kitesurfing. The bay offers side-shore winds (which push you toward the beach, not out to sea), flat water, and a gradual sandy entry. It's significantly safer than many other popular kite spots.
